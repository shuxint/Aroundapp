About Around

Around (The Around Hub) is a live iOS application connecting New York City users with local creators, including photographers, DJs, and artists.

The platform enables users to discover, book, and pay for creative services, supported by an AI-powered assistant that matches users with available creators.

Internship Reflection

During my internship at Around Technologies, I contributed to machine learning integration, backend development, and product experimentation, focusing on improving search efficiency and supporting booking functionality.

What I Built

AI-Powered Search

Redesigned the LLM-based matching pipeline to separate intent extraction from local matching logic. Reduced LLM calls from two to one per search, improving response latency and reducing unnecessary inference overhead.

A/B Testing

Evaluated model-driven product changes through experimentation and performance analysis.

Booking Backend

Contributed to calendar integration, scheduling functionality, and backend workflows supporting creator bookings.
